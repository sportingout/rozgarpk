About Rozgar PK

Rozgar PK is a platform designed to connect skilled workers directly with customers across Pakistan. Whether you need a Tile Mason, Electrician, Plumber, Painter, Carpenter, or AC Technician, Rozgar PK makes hiring simple and transparent.


Our goal is to empower skilled workers in cities like Multan, Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Faisalabad, and beyond by creating steady earning opportunities without any middleman commission.
